Terminology — IVR, VR/VE, AR

IVR

IVR – If VR is fully immersive with natural interactions, this meets the guidelines for full VR as set down by Brenda Laurel:

What is Virtual Reality?

https://medium.com/@blaurel/what-is-virtual-reality-77b876d829ba

Open website in a new tab

VR, VE

VR, VE – Virtual Reality and Virtual Environment:

These are somewhat synonymous, though the VE may not be as fully featured.

Virtual Reality is used indiscriminately and may not be accurate in all cases (360 video is sometimes incorrectly described as VR)

Virtual Reality is usually fully immersive

AR

AR – Augmented Reality involves enhancing a real world feed with virtual objects calibrated to the coordinate space.

On the Mixed Reality Scale, this can slide towards Augmented Virtuality – BBC Virtual News sets with real presenters

3DUI — Navigation

Navigation Classes
Navigation — Travel and Wayfinding

Navigation

The most common VE task, and is actually composed of two tasks.

Travel is the motor component of navigation, and just refers to the physical movement from place to place.

Wayfinding is the cognitive or decision making component of navigation, and ask the questions “Where am I”, ”Where do I want to go”, ”How do I get there”…

Interaction

Selection

Simply the specification of an object or

set of objects for some purpose.

Manipulation

Refers to the specification of object properties (most often position and orientation, but also other attributes). Selection and Manipulation are often used together, but selection may be a stand-alone task. For example, the user may select an object in order to apply a command such as delete to the obj.
